# Ritual: The Conversation

*U4b — dialogue intimacy. Emotions: curiosity · empathy · tension.*

## 1. Experience

The table is candlelit and close. When someone you know speaks inside the
GM's telling, their painted face appears beside the words — the tavern
keeper leans in, the villain's portrait darkens the margin. The story stops
being a wall of prose and becomes people talking to you.

## 2. What ships in U4b

- **Speaker portraits**: when a codex-known NPC's name appears near quoted
  speech in a finished GM beat, their portrait chip (the same face as the
  codex/journal) is seated at the bubble's left with name · role on hover.
  Heuristic, engine-side, zero LLM cost; first match wins.
- Existing intimacy kept: parchment GM bubbles, vellum player bubbles,
  dice moments interrupting as rituals of their own.

## 3. Deferred (matrix-tracked)

Relationship chips on companion lines (needs a relationship stat the engine
doesn't track yet) · choice-button dialogue moments (the input line is the
choice surface today).
